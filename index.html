About Aaf Lavandi Ka

Aid that keeps a paper trail — and a human face.

Aaf Lavandi Ka began with a single medical bill we helped a neighbour pay. Fifteen years later, we run six verified assistance programs across the region, each one reviewed by a real caseworker before a rupee ever moves. We believe relief should be fast, dignified, and easy to trace back to its source.

Every application, approval and disbursement is logged and available on request — because trust is earned one receipt at a time.
